HiveSight has three plans. Each one builds on the one below.

## Solo

**$99/month · 1 seat**

For founders running their own warm-intro motion. Everything you need to
map your own network, see warm paths, and stay sharp before meetings.

- Relationship mapping
- Warm intro path discovery
- Connection strength signals
- Google Calendar sync (pre-meeting dossiers)
- MCP access (query your network from Claude / Cursor / ChatGPT)

[Start a 7-day free trial](https://dashboard.hivesight.so) — no credit card
required.

## Team

- Everything in Solo

## What counts as a seat?

A seat is one person who can sign into the HiveSight app and have their
network mapped. Adding *manual* connectors (just a LinkedIn URL, no
sign-in) doesn't consume a seat. Connectors added via Google Calendar
enrichment also don't consume seats — they're soft connectors.

## What counts as an ecosystem?

Plans cap how many ecosystems a workspace can run — Solo workspaces are
scoped to a single ecosystem; Team and Enterprise lift that limit.
Most teams stay on one ecosystem regardless of plan — you'd spin up a
second when running a meaningfully different motion (new product, new
segment) that should be reported on independently.

## Billing

All paid plans are monthly. You can cancel anytime from **Settings →
Billing**. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing
period — your data and intro opportunities stay intact through that
window so you don't lose pipeline mid-cycle.
