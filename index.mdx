HiveSight is a relationship intelligence platform for revenue teams. It maps
who on your team actually knows who, scores how strong each connection is,
and surfaces the warmest paths into your target accounts — automatically.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your first ecosystem">
    An *ecosystem* is a workspace scoped to a single GTM motion — your ICP, your
    target accounts, and your team's connectors live inside it. Start with [the
    5-minute quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team's connectors">
    A connector is anyone on your team whose LinkedIn network you want to map.
    The more connectors you add, the more warm paths surface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define your ICP">
    Describe your ideal customer in plain English. HiveSight classifies every
    contact in your team's network against that definition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See your top intro paths">
    The dashboard ranks the strongest warm paths your team can run today —
    sorted by ICP fit × connection strength.
  </Step>
</Steps>
