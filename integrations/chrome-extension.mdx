title: "Chrome extension"
description: "Inline warm-path lookups and dossiers while your team browses LinkedIn and the web."
---

The HiveSight Chrome extension overlays warm-path information on the
sites your team already uses to research prospects — LinkedIn profiles,
LinkedIn company pages, and arbitrary email addresses surfaced by other
tools (HubSpot, Apollo, etc.).

## Install

1. Open the Chrome Web Store listing (linked from **Settings →
   Integrations → Chrome Extension**).
2. Click **Add to Chrome**.
3. Pin the extension to your toolbar for quick access.
4. Sign in with your HiveSight account on first launch.

## What it does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="LinkedIn profile overlay" icon="linkedin">
    On any LinkedIn profile, the extension shows whether your team has a
    warm path to this person, the strongest connector, ICP segment, and a
    quick "Save as opportunity" action.
  </Card>
  <Card title="LinkedIn company overlay" icon="building">
    On a LinkedIn company page, shows your team's coverage of that
    account — connector count, top warm paths, target-account status.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email lookup" icon="envelope">
    Right-click any email address on a webpage to look up the matching
    contact in HiveSight (warm paths + dossier).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inline dossier" icon="file-lines">
    Click any name surfaced by the extension to open the full HiveSight
    dossier without leaving the page.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Switching ecosystems

If you have access to multiple ecosystems, the extension popup has an
ecosystem switcher at the top. Pick the ecosystem whose graph you want
the inline overlays to query.

## Privacy

The extension only sends queries when you're on a supported page and
HiveSight has data to show. It doesn't track your browsing, doesn't read
page content beyond the LinkedIn profile/company identifier, and doesn't
sync anything back without explicit action (like saving an opportunity).
